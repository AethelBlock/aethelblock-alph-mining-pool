# Alephium (ALPH) Mining Pool — AethelBlock

Mine Alephium with AethelBlock using Blake3. Choose PPLNS to receive a share of rewards from blocks found by the pool, or SOLO to earn the reward when your miner finds a valid block, subject to the pool fee.

[Alephium setup guide and dashboard](https://www.aethelblock.com/alephium?lang=en) · [All AethelBlock mining pools](https://github.com/AethelBlock/aethelblock-mining-pools)

## Connection settings

| Mode | Stratum endpoint | Standard fee | Minimum payout |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| PPLNS | `alph-pool.aethelblock.com:3072` | 1% | 0.1 ALPH |
| SOLO | `alph-solo.aethelblock.com:3073` | 1% | 0.1 ALPH |

## Connect your miner

1. Choose hardware and mining software that support Blake3 on Alephium.
2. Select PPLNS or SOLO and enter the matching endpoint. Use your public ALPH receiving address and the worker and protocol settings from the linked setup guide.
3. Start mining and check for accepted shares in your miner log. Open the matching pool dashboard and look up your receiving address to monitor your worker.
